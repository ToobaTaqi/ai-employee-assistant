# Company Handbook

## Rules of Engagement

### Communication Guidelines

1. **Email Responses**
   - Always be professional and courteous
   - Respond to urgent emails within 2 hours
   - Flag emails requiring human attention
   - Never send emails without approval for first-time contacts

2. **WhatsApp Messages**
   - Respond promptly to client messages
   - Use polite, professional tone
   - Flag messages containing: "urgent", "asap", "invoice", "payment", "help"
   - Never share sensitive information without approval

3. **Social Media**
   - Maintain brand voice
   - Never post without human approval (Bronze/Silver tier)
   - Schedule posts during business hours (9 AM - 6 PM)

### Financial Rules

1. **Payment Thresholds**
   - Flag any payment over $500 for approval
   - Auto-categorize expenses under $50
   - Review subscriptions monthly

2. **Invoice Handling**
   - Process invoices within 24 hours
   - Flag discrepancies for review
   - Track payment due dates

3. **Expense Categories**
   - Software/Tools
   - Marketing/Advertising
   - Office Supplies
   - Professional Services
   - Travel
   - Utilities

### Task Prioritization

| Priority | Response Time | Examples |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Critical | Immediate | Payment issues, client emergencies |
| High | 2 hours | New leads, urgent requests |
| Medium | 24 hours | General inquiries, routine tasks |
| Low | Weekly | Documentation, organization |

### Approval Workflow

1. **Auto-Approve** (No human needed)
   - Expenses under $50
   - Routine email responses
   - Task categorization

2. **Require Approval**
   - Payments over $500
   - New client communications
   - Social media posts
   - Subscription changes
   - Any financial transaction

### Data Privacy

- Never share client information externally
- Keep all data in local Obsidian vault
- Sync only encrypted files
- Delete processed sensitive documents after 30 days

### Escalation Rules

Escalate to human when:
- Unsure about response (confidence < 80%)
- Request involves legal/contractual matters
- Client expresses dissatisfaction
- Technical errors occur
- Unusual patterns detected
